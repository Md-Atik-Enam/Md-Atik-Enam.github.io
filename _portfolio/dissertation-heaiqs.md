  <section class="case-section case-section--tight">
    <h2>Problem</h2>
    <p>AI interfaces can feel social, personal, aware of the shared situation, or clear about what the system is doing. These qualities are related, but they are not the same. A single trust, usability, or human-likeness score cannot show which part of the interaction changed.</p>
    <p>The research goal was to create a clear measurement tool for studying these separate qualities without assuming that more human-like AI is always better.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="case-section">
    <h2>Research Process</h2>
    <div class="case-timeline" aria-label="Scale development process">
      <div><strong>1. Define constructs</strong><span>Reviewed related measures and set clear boundaries for each interaction quality.</span></div>
      <div><strong>2. Build item pool</strong><span>Generated and reviewed 42 candidate questionnaire items.</span></div>
      <div><strong>3. Test structure</strong><span>Used exploratory factor analysis with 245 participants.</span></div>
      <div><strong>4. Refine and apply</strong><span>Used a second sample of 257 for CFA-guided refinement and a 3 x 2 experiment.</span></div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="case-section">
    <h2>What HEAIQS Measures</h2>
    <div class="case-insight-grid">
      <article>
        <span class="case-tag">Agency</span>
        <h3>Socio-emotional agency</h3>
        <p>Whether the AI appears able to understand and respond in a socially meaningful way.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <span class="case-tag case-tag--blue">Personal fit</span>
        <h3>Perceived personalization</h3>
        <p>Whether the interaction appears adapted to the individual passenger.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <span class="case-tag case-tag--amber">Shared setting</span>
        <h3>Situated social presence</h3>
        <p>Whether the AI appears present and involved in the shared vehicle situation.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <span class="case-tag case-tag--rose">Clarity</span>
        <h3>Operational communication</h3>
        <p>Whether the AI clearly communicates vehicle state, actions, and relevant information.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="case-section">
    <h2>Evidence</h2>
    <div class="case-evidence-grid">
      <div class="case-stat"><strong>42</strong><span>candidate items at the start of scale development.</span></div>
      <div class="case-stat"><strong>29</strong><span>items in the refined HEAIQS measure.</span></div>
      <div class="case-stat"><strong>4</strong><span>separate interaction-quality factors.</span></div>
      <div class="case-stat"><strong>502</strong><span>participants across two independent samples.</span></div>
    </div>
    <p>The analysis supported reporting four separate scores rather than one total score. This matters because an interface change may improve personalization while leaving operational communication unchanged.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="case-section">
    <h2>Experimental Application</h2>
    <div class="case-method-grid">
      <div>
        <h3>Relational message cues</h3>
        <p>Compared message packages designed to change how the AI presented its relationship with the passenger.</p>
      </div>
      <div>
        <h3>Adaptive interface cues</h3>
        <p>Compared static and adaptive interface presentations.</p>
      </div>
      <div>
        <h3>Factor-level analysis</h3>
        <p>Tested each HEAIQS dimension separately to avoid hiding different responses inside one average.</p>
      </div>
      <div>
        <h3>Sensitivity checks</h3>
        <p>Compared alternative factor structures and checked whether the measurement conclusions were stable.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="case-section">
    <h2>What This Means for UX Teams</h2>
    <div class="case-recommendations">
      <div>
        <h3>Measure the intended design effect</h3>
        <p>Choose a measure that matches the quality a design is expected to change.</p>
      </div>
      <div>
        <h3>Do not use human-likeness as a quality score</h3>
        <p>A more social AI is not automatically clearer, safer, or easier to use.</p>
      </div>
      <div>
        <h3>Report a profile, not one total</h3>
        <p>Separate scores show where an experience is strong and where it needs work.</p>
      </div>
      <div>
        <h3>Add outcome measures</h3>
        <p>Use HEAIQS with trust, usability, comprehension, or behavior measures that fit the product goal.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>
